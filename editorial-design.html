Editorial Design

This is a magazine designed as a major coursework project, themed around the 2024 Paris Olympic Games. I was responsible for the editorial concept, layout design, and visual presentation, combining text, photography, and graphic design to create a cohesive reading experience. Several photographs featured in the magazine were taken by myself, contributing an original visual perspective to the publication.
